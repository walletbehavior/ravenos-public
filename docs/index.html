01

Four workspaces. One intelligence index. One selected market.

The instrument—not its chain, venue, or provider—is the organizing object. Context follows the user from discovery through research and portfolio review.

Terminal

Companies, events, options, peers, sectors, and cross-asset context where current data is available.

03

Raven explains the behavior. Atlas explains the world around it.

They are presented as one decision-support system, with facts, interpretations, uncertainty, and unavailable data kept distinct.

Raven

Why now

Current evidence, behavioral changes, participation, recurring groups, path state, earlier reads, and measured outcomes. Deeper evidence remains inspectable without becoming the first thing a trader must learn.

Atlas

What surrounds the move

Company and instrument context, events, options, peers, sectors, sessions, and cross-market relationships—only where current verified context exists.

Combined view

One readable answer

Current facts, Raven interpretation, Atlas context, evidence maturity, uncertainty, and missing information remain distinct instead of being blended into one confidence claim.

Evidence first

The data stays in control

A written summary cannot create evidence, make an early read look proven, replace stale data, switch markets, or authorize a transaction.

04

Current reads and later proof are different products.

RavenOS keeps leading observations, historical context, and measured followthrough separate so the interface cannot look certain merely because data is plentiful.

Current

Opportunity

A timestamped decision context from Raven's current opportunity feed. It is not a ranked trade, order, or personalized plan.

Participation

Behavior

Aggregate actors and cohorts with declared samples and privacy boundaries. Recurrence does not imply identity, ownership, coordination, or causality.

Recurrence

Memory

How often recent condition families appear and persist. Frequency is context; it is not historical similarity or a forecast.

Historical

Similar history

Raven compares only information that was knowable at the time with what happened afterward. If that history cannot be verified, no comparison is shown.

Measured

Followthrough

What happened after the declared validation window. Confirmed, mixed, insufficient, and invalidated results stay visible.

Recency

Stale means stale

A stale or missing current read is never silently replaced by an old snapshot. Earlier information is labeled clearly when it is shown.

06

One economic portfolio. Many custody sources.

The user asks what the portfolio looks like. RavenOS can eventually normalize wallets, venues, and brokers beneath that question without pretending they are the same account.

Reporting value

USD or USDC equivalent

Every conversion must carry a timestamp and source. USD and USDC are not treated as risklessly identical.

Actual custody

Preserved underneath

Tradier cash remains broker USD. Hyperliquid collateral retains venue rules. On-chain holdings remain in their actual wallets and chains.

Normalization

No double counting

Account equity, child holdings, collateral, positions, wrapped assets, bridges, and duplicate wallet imports require explicit precedence and deduplication.

Current state

Connected portfolios are not open yet

Accounts and saved markets are available. Verified wallet and broker connections, along with saved portfolio history, are not yet active. RavenOS does not substitute a demo balance.

07

Your account, wallets, paid access, and trading permissions stay separate.

RavenOS uses managed sign-in and sessions you can review and revoke. Signing in never grants wallet or trading permission.

Identity

Managed Google and email access

Account sign-in is handled through managed Google, password, or one-time-code authentication. RavenOS does not receive your Google password.

Sessions

Reviewable and revocable

Account sessions expire, can be reviewed from your account, and can be revoked when a device should no longer have access.

Wallets

Connect is not authentication

A wallet must be verified and linked through a single-use standardized challenge before it can attach to an account. It still does not grant transaction authority.

Transactions

Intent-bound before signing

Any future transaction must match the reviewed user, wallet, chain, instrument, amount, route, fees, slippage, expiry, and unique intent record.

Trading safetyCustomer signing and order submission stay off until the complete trading path has passed independent security review.

RavenOS never asks for seed phrases or private keys.

08

Unavailable is a product state, not an empty card.

A feature appears only when Raven can verify the exact market, data, and freshness it needs. Otherwise the interface explains what is missing.

Working now

Live read-only intelligence

Current opportunities, Hyperliquid market data, exact crypto charts, Raven Reads, measured followthrough, aggregate participation, and current public evidence.

Limited

Atlas and exact spot coverage

Atlas shows only current listings and market context that RavenOS can display publicly. Spot coverage varies by chain, market, and available source.

Account

Accounts and saved markets are available

Paid plans, wallet and broker connections, and saved portfolio history are not open yet. Raven alerts appear only for accounts where they are available.

Separately authorized

Signing and submission

Wallet signatures, broker orders, transaction broadcast, confirmed trading, and customer position monitoring require a later security and execution milestone.
