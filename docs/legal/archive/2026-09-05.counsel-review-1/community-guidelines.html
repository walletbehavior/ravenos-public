Reward process—not outrage.

A proposed code for pseudonymous, evidence-centered profiles, trader discovery, constructive recognition, and public research.

01

A professional, non-toxic market community

Raven Community exists to help people find traders and research demonstrating consistent process, risk control, reproducible evidence, and honest uncertainty. It is not designed to reward rage, provocation, gambling, leverage, trade count, account-size flexing, or one lucky return.

Boards use transparent evidence thresholds and keep popularity separate from performance quality. No profile, follow, recognition, ranking, or Copy button is an endorsement by RavenOS.

02

Pseudonymous identity and privacy

Use your chosen Raven username. Do not impersonate a person, organization, Raven employee, wallet owner, or public figure. Do not falsely claim that a wallet, strategy, credential, affiliation, or result belongs to you.

Public participation is opt-in. Respect another user’s settings. Do not reveal or attempt to link private Raven-user data, email, legal identity, location, account balance, wallet association, Copy allocation, or other nonpublic information.

A public-chain address is public data, but connecting it to a natural person can create safety and privacy risk. Do not dox, threaten, stalk, or organize harassment around wallet activity.

03

Represent evidence honestly

Label live, simulated, shadow, reconstructed, source, and follower results accurately. Do not crop, edit, fabricate, cherry-pick, or omit material context to create a misleading performance claim. Do not present marked value as an executable exit or unknown cost basis as free inventory.

State material limitations, conflicts, compensation, positions, and uncertainty. Correct meaningful errors. Do not call an asset, trader, strategy, wallet, protocol, or contract “safe,” guaranteed, verified, or Raven-approved when the evidence does not support that exact claim.

04

Be constructive

No harassment, threats, hateful conduct, sexual exploitation, targeted humiliation, dogpiling, discrimination, or encouragement of self-harm. Critique evidence and methods rather than attacking people.

RavenOS does not provide public dislike counts, quote-dunking, engagement streaks, or unrestricted direct messaging. Do not recreate those dynamics through profile names, coordinated activity, or external campaigns directed at Raven users.

05

No scams, credential theft, or dangerous instructions

Never request or share seed phrases, private keys, recovery material, passwords, bearer tokens, or remote device access. No phishing, malware, fake support, impersonated wallet prompts, address poisoning, malicious contracts, or links designed to steal funds or identity.

Do not encourage a user to bypass policy, hide their location, evade sanctions, disable security, send funds for “verification,” or sign data they do not understand. RavenOS support will not ask for a seed phrase or private key.

06

No manipulation or deceptive coordination

Do not use Community to manipulate a market; coordinate undisclosed pumps, dumps, wash trading, fake volume, or front-running; spread knowingly false claims; conceal a material token or wallet relationship; or recruit users into fraudulent investments.

Clustered activity and wallet lead/lag are observations, not proof of insider status or copying. Do not accuse people of crimes or coordination based only on Raven analytics.

07

Disclose promotion and referrals

Clearly disclose compensation, referral benefits, free access, token allocations, employment, investments, family ties, and other material connections near the relevant claim or link. Do not hide the disclosure in a profile or assume readers know.

No spam, repetitive referral posting, fake testimonials, engagement buying, account farms, undisclosed sponsored research, or guaranteed-return claims. Referral participation does not grant special ranking or evidence status.

08

Following and positive recognition

Follow profiles because you want meaningful updates. Use the constrained “Useful” recognition for work that genuinely helped you understand evidence or process. Do not trade recognition, automate it, pressure others for it, or use it to imply performance verification.

Recognition contributes little or nothing to performance ranking. Follower count does not make a trader safer, more skilled, or more copyable.

09

Moderation and enforcement

RavenOS may limit visibility, remove content, disable public settings, restrict following or recognition, suspend features, preserve evidence, or suspend an account when reasonably necessary to enforce these Guidelines, protect users, investigate abuse, comply with law, or secure the service.

Enforcement should consider severity, intent, history, harm, evidence, and the risk of recurrence. The approved program must define notice, emergency action, and appeal processes where appropriate. Account or Community enforcement must not by itself trap assets in an embedded wallet.

10

Reporting and updates

The audited product does not yet expose a complete user-reporting and moderation console. That is a launch dependency before open user publishing expands. Until then, use the support channel shown in RavenOS and never include private keys or recovery material.

These Guidelines are versioned. Material changes may require acknowledgement before increasing public exposure. Users must always be able to make a profile private without first accepting new publication terms.
