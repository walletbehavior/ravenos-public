01

Program state and separate agreement

RavenOS currently lets eligible accounts create a referral code and records bounded, one-time attribution when another user voluntarily applies it. No referral reward, commission, subscription credit, entitlement, or payout is active in the audited build.

If RavenOS launches compensation, participation will require explicit acceptance of an effective Affiliate Program version. Creating an account, following a profile, or sharing an ordinary link does not enroll a user in a compensated program.

02

Eligibility and enrollment

A proposed affiliate must have an active Raven account and username, satisfy age, geography, sanctions, tax, fraud, and payment-verification requirements, and provide accurate program information. RavenOS may approve, decline, suspend, or limit enrollment based on risk and applicable law.

Enrollment does not create employment, agency, partnership, fiduciary, franchise, joint venture, or authority to bind RavenOS. An affiliate is responsible for its own content, channels, disclosures, registrations, and legal obligations.

03

Referral attribution

Referral attribution is linked to the code a new or eligible user voluntarily claims. Current controls reject self-referral and conflicting repeat attribution. Cookies, browsers, privacy tools, earlier referrals, support decisions, invalid traffic, account deletion, and technical failure can affect attribution.

The approved program must define the attribution window, first- or last-touch rule, eligible products, qualifying customer, duplicate handling, reversals, and dispute deadline. This candidate does not promise credit for every visit, signup, or purchase.

04

Compensation and qualification

No current referral activity earns compensation. Before a compensated program starts, RavenOS must publish the exact rate or schedule, eligible Raven Pro event, holding or refund period, minimum payout, payout method, currency, timing, expiration, chargeback and refund treatment, tax documentation, and jurisdictional limits.

Projected or example affiliate earnings are not guaranteed. RavenOS may withhold or reverse amounts associated with refunds, chargebacks, fraud, self-referral, duplicate or fake accounts, prohibited traffic, sanctions, legal restrictions, or material breach, subject to the approved terms and applicable law.

05

Clear disclosure of material connections

If you receive or may receive money, subscription credit, free service, discounts, access, or another benefit for a referral, you must clearly and conspicuously disclose that connection near the endorsement and referral link. Do not rely solely on a profile biography, platform tool, hidden hashtag, or vague phrase.

Plain-language example: “I may earn a commission if you subscribe to Raven Pro through this link.”

Your disclosure must be understandable on the device and in the medium used. You are responsible for updating it when the relationship changes.

06

Truthful product and performance claims

You may describe features accurately using current RavenOS materials. You may not promise profits, imply risk-free trading, claim guaranteed copy results, present reconstructed or simulated performance as live, hide material fees, state that RavenOS endorses a token or trader, or make regulatory, custody, security, or availability claims RavenOS has not approved.

Personal experience must be genuine and current. Unusual or unrepresentative results must not be presented as what users should generally expect. A referral relationship is not an investment recommendation by RavenOS.

07

Prohibited promotion

Do not use spam, purchased or stolen lists, unsolicited bulk messages, deceptive ads, fake reviews, impersonation, misleading domains, trademark bidding without permission, cookie stuffing, forced redirects, malware, bots, account farms, self-referrals, hidden frames, incentive abuse, fabricated urgency, harassment, or unlawful financial promotion.

Do not target minors, restricted persons, or locations where the promotion or product is unavailable. Do not publish confidential wallet or Raven-user information. Community referral links remain subject to the Community Guidelines.

08

Quality review, records, and audits

RavenOS may review public promotional placements, traffic quality, attribution, subscription status, refunds, and compliance with disclosures. Any monitoring must remain proportionate and be described in the Privacy Policy.

Affiliates may be required to provide representative placements or records reasonably necessary to investigate an issue. RavenOS can require correction or removal of inaccurate or noncompliant content.

09

Taxes and payment information

Affiliates are responsible for taxes, registrations, and reporting that apply to compensation. RavenOS may require tax forms, identity or business verification, and payment details before payout and may report compensation as required by law.

The approved program must identify the paying entity and payment processor and explain retention and protection of tax and payout data.

10

Suspension, termination, and outstanding amounts

Either party may end participation under the process stated in the approved version. RavenOS may suspend codes or payouts during a good-faith fraud, sanctions, payment, or compliance review. Ending the program does not erase valid audit or tax records.

The final terms must define treatment of pending, approved, disputed, reversed, and de minimis amounts. No outstanding amount exists merely because the current attribution system recorded a referral.

11

Program changes and review items

RavenOS may change a future program prospectively with required notice. Material changes can require reacceptance. Rates must not be changed retroactively for already-qualified events except as expressly permitted for fraud, refunds, or chargebacks.

Before activation, counsel must approve the contracting and paying entity, eligible jurisdictions, advertising and financial-promotion rules, program economics, cancellation and refund linkage, tax reporting, privacy disclosures, dispute terms, and anti-fraud controls.
