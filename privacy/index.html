RavenOS / Privacy

Your account is not your wallet.

This policy explains the limited information RavenOS uses to create and protect an account. Signing in does not give RavenOS custody, link a wallet, or authorize a trade.

01

Scope

This policy applies to the RavenOS market-intelligence workspace and RavenOS account service. Third-party exchanges, wallets, brokers, data providers, and authentication providers operate under their own privacy terms when you interact with them.

RavenOS currently separates identity from custody and execution. Creating an account does not prove ownership of a wallet or brokerage account and does not permit transaction signing or submission.

02

Information RavenOS collects

Account information

When you create or access an account, RavenOS receives a verified email address, an optional display name, the managed provider’s user identifier, and the authentication method used. RavenOS does not receive your Google password and does not retain Google access or refresh tokens.

Session and security information

RavenOS records random internal account and session identifiers, coarse device labels, authentication and activity times, expiry and revocation state, and bounded security-event records. For abuse prevention, network addresses are reduced to a coarse prefix and transformed with a one-way keyed hash before rate-limit storage.

Workspace and market requests

RavenOS and its infrastructure may process the pages, instruments, contracts, pools, timeframes, and market-data requests needed to provide the service and diagnose availability. When Saved Monitor is explicitly activated for an account, it stores canonical exact-market identity plus the selected timeframe, allowlisted indicators, Raven overlays, density, panel, schema version, and revision. If the separately entitled Raven Monitor beta is explicitly enabled, RavenOS may also store an owner-selected list of bounded evidence-change categories, normalized before-and-after evidence classifications, qualified source and detection times, plain-text explanations, limitations, read state, retention expiry, and exact-market deep-link context. It does not store raw provider responses, chart-plan prices, wallet information, cohort identities, positions, orders, signing material, or execution objects. Saved scans, linked wallets, brokerage connections, billing, customer trading, and out-of-app alert delivery are not active account features unless the product explicitly says otherwise.

Information RavenOS does not request

RavenOS does not ask for seed phrases, recovery phrases, wallet private keys, or brokerage passwords. A wallet address or public market-account address may be observable public data, but it is not treated as proof that you own or control it.

03

How information is used

RavenOS uses account and technical information to authenticate users, create and revoke secure sessions, prevent abuse, operate and troubleshoot the service, preserve product integrity, and comply with applicable obligations. RavenOS does not use a Google identity to obtain unrelated Google account data.

Market-workspace activity may be used in aggregated or de-identified form to improve reliability, coverage, and product design. RavenOS does not sell account credentials or seed phrases and cannot sell information it never collects.

04

Providers and disclosures

RavenOS uses service providers to operate authentication, hosting, security, and market-data functions. WorkOS manages hosted account authentication; Google provides Google sign-in when selected; Cloudflare provides edge delivery, serverless processing, and account-session storage.

Information is disclosed only as needed to operate those functions, protect users or the service, complete a requested integration, comply with law, or address a business transfer. RavenOS does not enable a wallet, broker, or trading connection merely because an account exists.

05

Retention and your choices

Active session records are retained for security and session management until they expire or are revoked. Account identity and bounded security-event records are retained while the account is active and as reasonably necessary for security, legal, and operational purposes.

When Saved Monitor is active, saved exact markets remain until you remove an individual item, unwatch the current market, or use “Delete all saved research state.” If the Raven Monitor beta is enabled, in-app transition notifications have a 90-day retention expiry and remain subject to a bounded account ceiling. “Delete notification history” removes notification evidence while keeping monitor rules. “Delete all alert research state” removes every monitor rule and notification owned by the signed-in customer while preserving saved markets. Deleting a saved market or account cascades through its monitor state. Database backups and security records follow separate limited operational or legal retention schedules and are not used to recreate deleted research state in the active product.

You can sign out the current session and review or revoke other active sessions from the RavenOS account desk. Until full account self-service deletion is available, account or privacy requests can be directed to the user-support address displayed in the RavenOS managed sign-in flow.

06

Security boundaries

RavenOS uses managed authentication, exact callback URLs, one-time OAuth state, PKCE, host-only secure cookies, opaque server sessions, expiry, rotation, revocation, CSRF defenses, origin isolation, and server-side secret storage. No internet service can promise absolute security.

Identity, wallet ownership, entitlement, transaction authorization, signing, and submission remain separate permissions. If RavenOS introduces any of those capabilities, their data handling and controls will be described before activation.

07

Changes and contact

RavenOS may update this policy as the product and its legal obligations change. Material changes will be reflected by a revised effective date and, where appropriate, an in-product notice.

For privacy questions or requests, use the user-support address displayed by the RavenOS managed authentication service. Do not send passwords, seed phrases, private keys, or sensitive transaction material.
