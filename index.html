RavenOS reads public crypto market behavior across chains, breadth, liquidity, leverage, and rotation.

It shows where conditions are improving, where moves are failing, and where the public sample is still too thin to trust.

RavenOS does not tell you what to buy. It helps you see when the market is broad, selective, or fragile.
