05A

Raven Rewards: Pro fee cashback

Active paid Pro and Pro trial members earn 30% of the successfully collected Raven native execution fee charged to the customer, before any provider share, back in USDC-denominated value. This is a fee rebate, not interest, yield, investment return, or trading profit. Standard and Pro are both charged the actual 1.00% Raven fee. The quote shows the estimated rebate for the selected route. Raven funds the full rebate after any provider share, so the effective Raven execution fee after cashback is 0.70%. On a $1,000 eligible Jupiter trade with a $10 Raven fee, Jupiter retains $2, the Pro customer earns $3 cashback, and Raven retains $5 before other costs. Manual and copy trades use the same rules, with no extra copy surcharge. Hyperliquid fees are excluded from this cashback program.

A quote displays estimated cashback. It becomes earned and available only when Raven reconciles the successful execution and the collector’s actual fee receipt. Failed trades and missing or mismatched fee receipts do not earn available rewards. Non-USDC fee receipts require supported, evidenced USDC valuation; a bridged stablecoin is not silently treated as canonical USDC. Amounts use six-decimal micro-USDC accounting and round down; partial micro-USDC amounts are not credited.

Rewards do not expire. Trial expiry, Pro cancellation, and a return to Standard do not erase earned rewards. Available balances, pending estimates, reservations, claims, and subscription applications appear separately in Account. Raven maintains rewards as a liability against its fee economics. Rewards are not represented as assets already segregated in a wallet for each user, and fees collected on different chains do not imply a single pooled funding wallet.

When claims are available, you may request a canonical USDC claim to a supported Raven embedded wallet or a connected wallet verified as under your control. Network selection is explicit. The review shows the destination, network, minimum claim, and estimated network cost. On-chain claims are currently unavailable pending a funded settlement service. The supported claim policy is canonical USDC on Solana, Base, and Ethereum, subject to funding and network availability. Your balance remains available for subscription credit and does not expire while claims are unavailable. It does not substitute USDC.e, Binance-Peg USDC, or USDG. Raven covers the displayed network cost under the configured policy. A claim reserves funds first and is marked claimed only after independently verified settlement. Provider uncertainty may delay release; it is not proof of payment.

You may instead apply available rewards as USD invoice credit toward Raven Pro, at one USDC-denominated reward dollar per invoice dollar. Applications use whole cents; sub-cent rewards remain in the rewards balance. Stripe invoice credit, invoice value, and external payment are distinct records. A full credit can cover the current $149 invoice with no external charge, while explicit checkout may still collect a payment method for future renewals. Applying rewards alone does not create or renew a subscription. Credit already posted to Stripe remains on the dedicated Pro billing account if checkout is abandoned.

Automatic application is off by default. If you opt in, Raven may apply available rewards up to the next Pro renewal amount. It never uses pending rewards or creates a subscription merely because your balance reaches $149. You can turn the preference off at any time; turning it off does not reverse invoice credit already applied. A cancelled subscription does not keep initiating reward applications.

If Raven’s underlying execution fee is demonstrably refunded or reversed, Raven appends a reward reversal with evidence. Original history is retained. If the rebate was already spent, the outstanding adjustment is shown separately and may offset later earned rebates. Ordinary trading losses are not a reason for a reward reversal.

Trials are subject to server-side eligibility: one trial per account and verified authentication identity, retained trial history, prior paid history where known, and existing security restrictions. A wallet address alone does not establish eligibility. Existing-account trials require an explicitly configured policy. Multiple email identities remain an abuse vector; Raven does not use invasive device fingerprinting.
