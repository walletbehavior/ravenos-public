Straight answers

The basics, without the jargon.

What RavenOS does today, how to use it, and where the limits are.

Live market research Exact-market charts Research only Not financial advice
Is RavenOS financial advice?
No. RavenOS provides general market information and research tools. Raven Reads, rankings, alerts, scenarios, and chart levels are not personalized recommendations to buy, sell, hold, borrow, or use leverage. You are responsible for your own decisions and risk.
What is RavenOS?
RavenOS is a market-intelligence workspace for finding markets, opening the exact pool or contract, checking charts and current activity, reviewing holders and risk, and testing a setup with Raven’s behavioral evidence.
What is a Raven Read?
A Raven Read explains why Raven noticed a market, what changed, what supports the setup, what contradicts it, and what would weaken or invalidate it. Raven only names specific behavior when the attached data supports that claim.
What do Velocity, Raven, and Activity mean in Discover?
Velocity looks for meaningful acceleration. Raven shows only markets with a current qualified Raven observation. Activity ranks changes in participation rather than raw volume alone.
Why does RavenOS insist on an exact market?
The same ticker can exist on multiple chains, venues, and pools. RavenOS keeps the exact chain, venue, pool or contract, and quote asset attached so a chart, risk panel, holder list, or Raven Read is not silently taken from a different market.
What should I check before treating a move as actionable?
Review liquidity, routeable size, valuation versus liquidity, recent txns, holder concentration, developer exposure, token controls, project links, and Raven’s stated invalidation. A strong percentage move can still be a poor or dangerous market.
What does Raven Lab add?
Behavior Lab tests aggregate participation across chain, market-size, and time-window cohorts. Perps Intelligence adds funding, open interest, pressure, crowding, spread, and depth. These broader patterns add context; they do not prove that one wallet or cohort is involved in one exact market.
What does Atlas add?
Atlas provides listed-market charts, breadth presentation, SEC filings, insider activity, and broader market context where RavenOS has public display rights. Restricted or unsupported values remain unavailable.
Does RavenOS place trades today?
No. Current public tools inspect markets and review routes. They do not sign, submit, broadcast, or confirm an order. Any future trading flow must keep the exact market, route, fees, slippage, and final user confirmation visible.
What works without an account?
Public discovery, search, charts, Raven Reads, holders and market risk where available, Behavior Lab, Perps Intelligence, Atlas, and recent-market history on that browser. Recent browser history is local and does not sync between devices.
What does an account add?
An account can sync exact saved markets and their bounded chart setup across devices. Raven Monitor is available only where enabled for the account. Paid enrollment and connected wallet or broker history are not open yet.
What happens when data is missing or delayed?
RavenOS labels the view delayed or unavailable and keeps unrelated market tools working. It does not turn missing data into zero, silently switch to a similarly named pool, or present an old Raven setup as current.
Will RavenOS ever ask for my seed phrase or private key?
No. Never share a seed phrase, recovery phrase, or private key with RavenOS or anyone claiming to represent RavenOS.
