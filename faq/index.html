What is a Raven Read?

A Raven Read is an evidence-backed market interpretation showing what Raven is seeing, why it matters, and what would confirm or weaken the read.

Are Raven Reads trade instructions?

No. Raven Reads are not buy, sell, long, short, or execution instructions. They help focus attention on changing market behavior.

What does Terminal cover?

Terminal covers crypto-native chain and perps intelligence: Solana, Base, Ethereum, Perps, Hyperliquid context, Raven Reads, wallet/account state, and quote-only review.

What does Atlas cover?

Atlas is the broader macro and traditional market intelligence boundary: equities, ETFs, futures, rates, FX, commodities, energy, and broad risk regime.

What are Outcomes?

Outcomes track what happened after earlier Raven reads, including mixed, insufficient, negative, and still-forming results.

What are Read records?

Read records preserve evidence details behind earlier reads. They support followthrough checks, but are not a separate trading signal page.

Does RavenOS custody funds or trade for me?

No. Current public flows are intelligence, account context, and quote-only preview. Signing, submission, broadcasting, custody, and unattended execution are disabled.

Why can Opportunity and Outcomes disagree?

Opportunity is a leading read. Outcomes is lagging validation. A current setup can improve before settled evidence confirms it.
