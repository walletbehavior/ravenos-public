RavenOS / product boundaries

What Raven does. What Raven does not pretend to do.

RavenOS is a cross-market intelligence workspace with a real read-only market feed. These answers separate what works now from what remains unavailable.

Current Raven intelligence Live Hyperliquid data No customer account system No signing or submission
What is RavenOS?
RavenOS is one cross-market decision workspace. It combines discovery, exact-instrument market data, Raven behavioral evidence, Atlas market context, measured followthrough, and provider-specific review beneath one interface. It is currently read-only.
Do I choose Solana, perps, or equities before I search?
No. Search for the market you want. RavenOS resolves asset class, venue, chain, exact contract or pool, chart source, available intelligence, settlement, and provider compatibility. If a symbol is ambiguous, the product must ask—it cannot silently choose another market.
What is a Raven Read?
A Raven Read is a structured interpretation of current market facts and Raven evidence. It explains why Raven noticed a change, what supports it, what weakens it, the current path, and what remains unknown. It is not a buy, sell, long, short, or execution instruction.
What does Atlas add?
Atlas adds company, equity, ETF, option, event, peer, sector, session, and cross-asset context where current verified data exists. Atlas and Raven appear in one intelligence view; Atlas cannot invent behavioral evidence or make a stale Raven read current.
Why does exact instrument identity matter?
Because BTC spot is not BTC-PERP, a token is not an exact pool, SPY is an exact listing, and an option requires its underlying, expiry, strike, and right. Every chart, evidence join, quote preview, and future order must refer to the same exact object.
Why can a current signal and later followthrough disagree?
A current signal is a leading decision-time read. Followthrough measures what happened after the declared future window. A setup can look stronger now while earlier evidence remains mixed, insufficient, or invalidated. RavenOS keeps both states visible.
What is the difference between recurrence and similar history?
Recurrence measures how often a condition has appeared and persisted recently. Similar history requires an as-of reconstruction and future-only measurement for genuinely comparable earlier cases. If that history cannot be established, RavenOS shows it as unavailable rather than inventing similarity scores.
Does RavenOS execute trades today?
No. Current customer signing, order submission, transaction broadcast, confirmed trading, and position monitoring are unavailable. The interface may show market context and a clearly non-executable review boundary, but no control may imply that an order was placed.
How would stocks, ETFs, and options execute later?
Through a regulated broker. RavenOS can provide research, Atlas context, quote review, and orchestration; the broker remains responsible for the customer account, custody, legal settlement, and execution. RavenOS does not become the broker.
How would on-chain spot and perps execute later?
An exact Solana pool can resolve to a Jupiter-compatible route, while a Hyperliquid perpetual resolves to the venue’s order path and margin rules. The Terminal can stay consistent, but custody, settlement, and final user confirmation remain provider-specific.
Is there a unified Portfolio?
Not yet. A future view can combine verified wallets, Hyperliquid, Tradier, and other providers into one economic report while retaining actual custody and settlement. RavenOS will not show a demonstration balance as though it belongs to the user.
Are USD and USDC treated as the same?
No. A unified report may show a timestamped USD or USDC equivalent, but broker USD and on-chain USDC retain their actual custody, conversion source, recency, and risk. A missing or old conversion fails closed.
Does connecting a wallet sign me in?
No. Wallet connection, wallet verification, RavenOS sign-in, account linking, entitlement, and transaction approval are separate concepts. No production account or wallet-linking system is active yet.
Can I subscribe or create an account?
Not yet. Production authentication, server sessions, subscriptions, Stripe entitlements, persistent watchlists, alerts, and layouts remain unavailable. They will not be represented by client-only locks or placeholder account states.
What happens when current data is unavailable?
The affected surface fails closed or shows a clearly labeled archive. RavenOS does not silently replace a missing current opportunity with an old historical claim, and one provider outage should not collapse unrelated markets.
Is Raven Plan an executable trade plan?
No. Any Plan or Scenario Preview remains evidence-bound, research-only, non-personalized, and non-executable until the supporting evidence and safety controls prove otherwise. It must show sample size, maturity, observation time, and limitations. A plan can never authorize a transaction.
Does RavenOS ever need my seed phrase or private key?
No. RavenOS should never ask for, receive, or store seed phrases, recovery phrases, or private keys. A normal wallet keeps custody and performs any future final signature in its own confirmation interface.
