RavenOS / operator guide

Choose the market. Raven handles the plumbing.

RavenOS resolves exact instrument identity, live market data, Raven evidence, Atlas context, custody, settlement, and the appropriate provider beneath one workspace. This guide explains what is real now, what is staged, and where the product fails closed.

01

03

Raven explains the behavior. Atlas explains the world around it.

They are presented as one decision-support system, with facts, interpretations, uncertainty, and unavailable data kept distinct.

Raven

Why now

Current evidence, Behavioral Atoms, participation, cohorts, path state, earlier reads, and matured outcomes. Deeper lineage remains inspectable without becoming the first thing a trader must learn.

Atlas

What surrounds the move

Company and instrument context, events, options, peers, sectors, sessions, and cross-market relationships—only where a current bounded projection exists.

Shared sidecar

One readable answer

Current facts, Raven interpretation, Atlas context, evidence maturity, uncertainty, and missing information are rendered as separate structured fields—not arbitrary HTML.

Boundary

No narrator authority

Generated prose cannot create evidence, upgrade maturity, substitute a stale source, select another instrument, or authorize a transaction.

04

Current reads and later proof are different products.

RavenOS keeps leading observations, historical context, and measured followthrough separate so the interface cannot look certain merely because data is plentiful.

Current

Opportunity

A timestamped decision context from the current Opportunity Census. It is not a ranked trade, order, or personalized plan.

Participation

Behavior

Aggregate actors and cohorts with declared samples and privacy boundaries. Recurrence does not imply identity, ownership, coordination, or causality.

Recurrence

Memory

How often recent condition families appear and persist. Frequency is context; it is not historical similarity or a forecast.

Historical

Similar history

Requires a decision-time reconstruction and future-only measurements. If that lineage is unavailable, RavenOS shows no analogue rows.

Measured

Followthrough

What happened after the declared validation window. Confirmed, mixed, insufficient, and invalidated results stay visible.

Recency

Stale means stale

A stale or missing current projection is never silently replaced by an old snapshot. Current routes fail closed or label a verified archive explicitly.

06

One economic portfolio. Many custody sources.

The user asks what the portfolio looks like. RavenOS can eventually normalize wallets, venues, and brokers beneath that question without pretending they are the same account.

Reporting value

USD or USDC equivalent

Every conversion must carry a timestamp and source. USD and USDC are not treated as risklessly identical.

Actual custody

Preserved underneath

Tradier cash remains broker USD. Hyperliquid collateral retains venue rules. On-chain holdings remain in their actual wallets and chains.

Normalization

No double counting

Account equity, child holdings, collateral, positions, wrapped assets, bridges, and duplicate wallet imports require explicit precedence and deduplication.

Current state

Portfolio persistence unavailable

No customer account, verified connection, entitlement store, or persistent portfolio exists publicly. RavenOS shows an honest connection boundary, not a demo balance.

07

Account, wallet, entitlement, and transaction authority remain separate.

Customer security is a prerequisite—not a set of connected-looking controls. RavenOS uses OWASP ASVS 5.0.0 as the verification baseline for future account capability.

Identity

Managed passkeys and recovery

No home-grown password store. Future accounts use random internal IDs, managed WebAuthn/passkeys, protected recovery, session visibility, and revocation.

Sessions

Opaque and server-side

No long-lived bearer sessions in browser storage. Cookie sessions require rotation, expiry, CSRF defense, object authorization, and recent reauthentication for sensitive changes.

Wallets

Connect is not authentication

A wallet must be verified and linked through a single-use standardized challenge before it can attach to an account. It still does not grant transaction authority.

Transactions

Intent-bound before signing

Any future transaction must match the reviewed user, wallet, chain, instrument, amount, route, fees, slippage, expiry, and unique intent record.

Current security gateNo customer signing or order submission until the complete identity, session, authorization, abuse, and transaction-security path has independent review.

RavenOS never asks for seed phrases or private keys.

08

Unavailable is a product state, not an empty card.

A capability appears only when identity, source, recency, entitlement, and safety requirements are satisfied. Otherwise the interface explains what is missing.
