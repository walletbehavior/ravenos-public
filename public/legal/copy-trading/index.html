The source trade is a signal—not your result.

01

Copying does not reproduce the source wallet

Your trade can occur later, at a different price, in a different size, through a different route, with different fees and liquidity, or not at all. The source can profit while you lose. A profitable wallet can be impossible or uneconomic to copy at your size.

RavenOS detects public or connected-account activity, normalizes it, checks your settings and current evidence, requests a route, and may prepare an action. Every stage adds delay and can fail. RavenOS does not promise to detect, interpret, enter, scale, or exit every source action.

02

Mirror and Raven Copy are different

Mirror attempts to follow source behavior as closely as current constraints and safety boundaries allow. It is not exact replication.

Raven Copy treats a source action as evidence. RavenOS may copy, skip, require review, or mark it indeterminate based on exit availability, liquidity, friction, delay, instrument identity, capital, gas, portfolio exposure, and user policy.

The product must label the mode and preserve skipped signals. A skip can avoid a loss or miss a gain; neither outcome proves the policy was universally correct.

03

Source wallets can be unknown, wrong, or adversarial

A public address does not reveal the person or system controlling it. A Raven username is not automatically linked to a wallet. A signed challenge only proves control at the time of signing.

The source may change strategy, transfer instead of trade, trade privately, hedge elsewhere, receive tokens at unknown cost, enter through another wallet, manipulate followers, front-run observable copies, promote assets, or intentionally exit against followers. Public transfers are not automatically buys or sells.

Wallet P&L can be incomplete because deposits, withdrawals, airdrops, bridge activity, missing history, unknown cost basis, fees, and offchain activity change the result. Raven rankings and copyability are evidence, not endorsement.

04

Detection, price, liquidity, and execution gap

Source observation, decode, policy evaluation, quote creation, signing, submission, confirmation, and reconciliation take time. During that interval, price, reserves, order-book depth, gas, funding, route, and token controls may change.

Follower size matters. A $25 copy may execute while a $5,000 copy fails or materially moves the market. A source with preferential order placement, gas, private flow, launch access, or pre-positioned capital may obtain results unavailable to followers.

Entry and exit routes are separate. An asset that can be bought may become impossible or uneconomic to sell. RavenOS may require a current reverse-route check, but a successful exit preview is not a guarantee of future liquidity.

05

Chain-local funding and Universal USDC

USDC on Solana, Ethereum, Base, Robinhood Chain, BNB Chain, or another venue remains a separate balance and representation. One displayed economic total is not one instantly spendable wallet balance.

Fast Copy strategies may require pre-positioned USDC and native gas on the source chain or venue. Moving funds across chains adds delay, cost, stablecoin, bridge, solver, finality, and failure risk. A target price can disappear while funding moves.

Autonomous bridging is disabled in the audited build. Any later CapitalTransferIntent must remain separate from a trade, identify the route and risks, reconcile departure and arrival, and receive the approval required by current policy.

06

User policy, limits, and skipped trades

Raven Copy is designed to apply explicit limits such as allowed chains, venues, instruments, notional, capital allocation, slippage, price impact, liquidity, gas reserve, exposure, turnover, drawdown, frequency, evidence freshness, and approval thresholds where implemented.

Limits reduce but do not eliminate risk. Splitting, correlation, rapidly changing quotes, provider failure, unexpected token behavior, and already-open exposure can produce different results. RavenOS may fail closed when evidence is unavailable or stale.

The model cannot weaken policy, sign, broadcast, select arbitrary recipients, retry an expired leg, or silently declare an incomplete plan complete. A policy allow is time-bound permission for the exact plan and evidence—not a general trading authorization.

07

Partial execution and non-atomic plans

Multi-leg and cross-venue plans are not atomic. One spot leg may fill while a hedge fails, expires, or returns an ambiguous response. You may be left with an unintended directional position, gas cost, fee, funding exposure, or stranded asset.

RavenOS must show partially executed and reconciliation-required states and identify unresolved exposure. Completion of one leg does not automatically authorize another after price, portfolio, policy, or evidence changes.

A retry, unwind, or compensation trade is a new risk decision. RavenOS must not automatically execute it unless the exact action was preauthorized within a reviewed policy and remains valid under current evidence.

08

Interactive signing and future delegation

Manual trading requires the user to review and authorize the transaction or order through the selected wallet. Live unattended Copy would require a separate constrained signing capability. It is not enabled merely because an embedded wallet exists.

If delegated signing is introduced, RavenOS must show the permitted chains, assets, router contracts, actions, maximum order size, maximum daily notional, expiration, and revocation controls. Withdrawal and arbitrary transfers must remain outside scope unless separately and conspicuously authorized.

Delegation increases compromise and implementation risk. Server policy is not the same as cryptographic enforcement. Any constraint that the wallet provider cannot enforce must be disclosed before authorization.

09

Historical, shadow, source, and live results

Historical reconstruction estimates prior activity from available evidence. Shadow or simulated performance models actions that were not executed with user money. Source performance belongs to the observed source. Follower performance is the actual or simulated result after follower delay, policy, size, route, and costs. Live performance requires observed fills and reconciliation.

These categories must not be blended. Hypothetical results can benefit from hindsight, idealized evidence, missing failures, incomplete costs, or selection bias. Prospective shadow evidence is more informative than reconstruction but is still not a fill.

Copyability can vary by follower size and over time. “Insufficient evidence” must remain visible rather than converted to a score or zero.

10

Fees and performance drag

Copy uses the same applicable execution-fee policy as manual trading and has no separate Copy surcharge: 1.00% for Standard and Raven Pro on supported native execution when the fee is enabled and displayed. Active Pro and Pro trial users receive the same 30% fee cashback as manual traders, only after the actual Raven fee is collected and reconciled. Copy trading itself does not require Pro; advanced copyability intelligence does. Hyperliquid constraints differ by instrument.

Network, priority, venue, protocol, router, solver, bridge, gas, funding, spread, slippage, price impact, and token fees are additional. Repeated small trades can be dominated by costs. Source-wallet results may not include the costs a follower pays.

11

Pause, kill, expiration, and remaining exposure

Pausing or killing a Copy specification should prevent new authorized activity after the control is processed. It may not cancel an already submitted transaction, close an open position, recover a pending bridge, or remove token approvals.

Ending Copy does not automatically sell positions. You remain responsible for reviewing open, pending, partially executed, and unreconciled exposure. Provider outages and chain conditions can delay a stop command.

Withdrawal, wallet export, and recovery must remain distinct from accepting new Copy terms or maintaining a Pro subscription.

12

Proposed first-live-Copy acknowledgement

Before first live Copy activation, RavenOS should require an unchecked, server-recorded acceptance bound to this exact version and content hash, in addition to the current Terms, Privacy Policy, and Trading Risk Disclosure.

Acknowledgement: I understand that copying does not reproduce a source wallet’s price, execution, or performance; RavenOS may skip or fail to detect trades; funding, fees, liquidity, delay, and my settings can materially change results; partial execution can leave unhedged exposure; and no source wallet or Raven ranking is guaranteed or endorsed.

Accepting this disclosure does not activate live Copy, delegated signing, or autonomous bridging. Each requires separate technical and product authorization.
