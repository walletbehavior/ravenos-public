Program state and separate agreement

Raven Referrals compensates participating affiliates for qualifying, voluntarily purchased Raven Pro software subscriptions. Ordinary Raven users do not need to join or accept these Affiliate Terms. Joining requires an active Raven account, a username, and explicit acceptance of the current effective Affiliate Terms. Raven records the accepted version, content hash, and acceptance time.

Eligible new customers receive the normal 30-day Pro trial with no card required. Trial expiry does not authorize a charge. Referral links do not add trial time, cash bonuses, or trading discounts. The customer must separately choose a paid subscription.

02

Eligibility and enrollment

An affiliate applicant must have an active Raven account and username, satisfy age, geography, sanctions, tax, fraud, and payment-verification requirements, and provide accurate program information. RavenOS may approve, decline, suspend, or limit enrollment based on risk and applicable law.

Enrollment does not create employment, agency, partnership, fiduciary, franchise, joint venture, or authority to bind RavenOS. An affiliate is responsible for its own content, channels, disclosures, registrations, and legal obligations.

Attribution through the free trial

The first qualifying referral visit establishes a 60-day attribution window. The relationship is locked when signup begins and saved with the Raven account. Later links cannot replace it. Server-side attribution survives logout, device changes, cookie deletion after signup, and trial expiry. Stable referral codes identify the affiliate independently of a mutable username.

The first qualifying paid Pro conversion must occur before that attribution window ends. A customer who signs up on day 0 and voluntarily pays on day 40 remains eligible. Raven rejects self-referral and duplicate attribution. A visit or signup is not a promise of commission, and technical failures before server-side attribution can prevent tracking.

Commission basis, duration, and reversals

The initial commission rate is 25% of actual net qualifying Raven Pro subscription cash revenue after Raven-issued rewards, credits, discounts, refunds, and chargebacks. At the current $149 monthly price, a fully qualifying payment produces $37.25 commission. If $49 of Rewards reduces the external payment to $100, commission is $25. A fully reward-funded invoice produces no commission.

The commission window runs for 12 calendar months from the first qualifying paid conversion, not from the click, signup, or trial start. A full-reward month preserves attribution within an established window. Cancellation stops future commission when qualifying revenue stops. Resubscribing during the original window may resume commission but never restarts the clock. Twelve continuous qualifying $149 payments would nominally produce $447; continuation, eligibility, and payment are not guaranteed.

Commissions enter a pending ledger and require review before becoming available for payout. Refunds or chargebacks create permanent reversal entries. An already-paid commission becomes a visible recoverable adjustment that may offset future commission, subject to these Terms and applicable law. Original ledger records are never deleted. Trading losses do not create affiliate adjustments.

No commission is paid for clicks, accounts, trials, wallet funding, trades, execution fees, Copy activity, follower counts, deposits, P&L, or cashback. Hyperliquid referral or integrator arrangements are separate.

05

Clear disclosure of material connections

If you receive or may receive money, subscription credit, free service, discounts, access, or another benefit for a referral, you must clearly and conspicuously disclose that connection near the endorsement and referral link. Do not rely solely on a profile biography, platform tool, hidden hashtag, or vague phrase.

Plain-language example: “I earn a commission if you subscribe to Raven Pro through my referral link.”

Your disclosure must be understandable on the device and in the medium used. You are responsible for updating it when the relationship changes.

06

Truthful product and performance claims

You may describe features accurately using current RavenOS materials. You may not promise profits, imply risk-free trading, claim guaranteed copy results, present reconstructed or simulated performance as live, hide material fees, state that RavenOS endorses a token or trader, or make regulatory, custody, security, or availability claims RavenOS has not approved.

Personal experience must be genuine and current. Unusual or unrepresentative results must not be presented as what users should generally expect. A referral relationship is not an investment recommendation by RavenOS.

07

Prohibited promotion

Do not use spam, purchased or stolen lists, unsolicited bulk messages, deceptive ads, fake reviews, impersonation, misleading domains, trademark bidding without permission, cookie stuffing, forced redirects, malware, bots, account farms, self-referrals, hidden frames, incentive abuse, fabricated urgency, harassment, or unlawful financial promotion.

Do not target minors, restricted persons, or locations where the promotion or product is unavailable. Do not publish confidential wallet or Raven-user information. Community referral links remain subject to the Community Guidelines.

Review, privacy, and program records

Raven may review public promotional placements, verified subscription payments, refunds, and compliance with disclosure rules. Disqualification uses recorded reasons such as self-referral, duplicate attribution, refunded or failed payment, fraudulent account, Terms violation, or manual review. Sharing an IP address, household, or funding wallet alone is not proof of abuse. This program does not introduce invasive fingerprinting.

Affiliate dashboards show aggregate visits, accounts, trials, paid conversions, and commission events. They do not expose a referred customer’s email, legal identity, wallet, balance, trades, Copy strategies, or cashback balance. Affiliate economics do not influence Community rank, trading reputation, or Copyability. A profile referral CTA includes a visible commission disclosure.

09

Taxes and payment information

Affiliates are responsible for taxes, registrations, and reporting that apply to compensation. RavenOS may require tax forms, identity or business verification, and payment details before payout and may report compensation as required by law.

Payouts initially require manual review and documented payment evidence. Automated payouts are unavailable. No payout provider, minimum, or schedule is promised. An available balance is an accounting balance, not evidence that a payout has been made. Raven will display the payment method, timing, and verification requirements before requesting payout authorization. Tax and payment details must be protected under the Privacy Policy.

10

Suspension, termination, and outstanding amounts

You may stop sharing your referral link at any time. RavenOS may end participation for a material breach of these Terms. RavenOS may suspend codes or payouts during a good-faith fraud, sanctions, payment, or compliance review. Ending the program does not erase valid audit or tax records.

Valid commission history remains recorded when participation ends. Pending and disputed amounts remain reviewable; refunds and chargebacks may create reversals or recoverable adjustments. A recorded referral alone does not create an earned balance.

11

Program changes

RavenOS may change a future program prospectively with required notice. Material changes can require reacceptance. Rates must not be changed retroactively for already-qualified events except as expressly permitted for fraud, refunds, or chargebacks.

Rewards remain separate

Pro cashback is a rebate of Raven’s own execution fee, accounted in USDC. Affiliate commissions are a separate USD subscription-revenue ledger. Applying Rewards to Pro creates an invoice credit and reduces qualifying affiliate revenue; it is not a new external payment. Neither a cashback credit nor a claim creates affiliate compensation. Native Terminal and Copy fees remain 1% for Standard and Pro, with 30% of confirmed Raven fees rebated to eligible Pro users and no additional Copy surcharge.
