Overview

RavenOS is a market intelligence and research platform. RavenOS provides tools for analyzing market structure, participation, pressure, replay patterns, liquidity conditions, market breadth, and cross-market context.

RavenOS is designed to help users understand market behavior. RavenOS does not provide investment advice, portfolio management, brokerage services, custody services, or trade execution.

No Brokerage or Trade Execution

RavenOS is not a broker-dealer. RavenOS does not execute trades, route orders, hold customer funds, hold customer assets, provide custody, or manage customer portfolios.

RavenOS provides software, analytics, and research tools only.

Research and Analytics Platform

RavenOS generates research outputs including but not limited to structure analysis, replay analysis, market context, participation analysis, pressure analysis, liquidity analysis, outcome studies, historical observations, alerts, and watchlists.

These outputs are analytical observations and should not be interpreted as predictions or guarantees.

Historical Analysis Limitations

RavenOS may compare current market structures to historical observations. Historical similarities do not guarantee future outcomes. Past observations are not necessarily indicative of future performance.

Markets evolve and conditions change. Historical replay tools should be viewed as research aids rather than forecasting systems.

Outcome and Replay Research

Certain RavenOS features may summarize historical outcome distributions associated with observed structures, including expansion, continuation, reversal, and failure.

These statistics are descriptive. They are not forecasts, probabilities of future outcomes, or guarantees. They are summaries of previously observed behavior.

Coverage Levels

RavenOS may display coverage states such as Public, Indexed, Deep Raven, Developing Coverage, and Limited Coverage.

Coverage levels describe research depth and data availability. Coverage levels do not imply accuracy, certainty, profitability, or investment merit.

Confidence Levels

RavenOS may display confidence indicators. Confidence reflects internal research characteristics such as sample depth, data quality, replay quality, coverage quality, and historical observation count.

Confidence is not certainty. High confidence does not guarantee a particular market outcome.

Experimental Features

Some RavenOS features may be designated as Experimental, Founder, Beta, or Developing. Experimental features are provided for research and testing purposes.

Experimental features may change without notice, produce incomplete results, be removed, contain errors, or produce inaccurate conclusions. Users should exercise additional caution when reviewing experimental outputs.

Alerts and Watchlists

Alerts and watchlists are informational tools. Alert generation does not imply a trading opportunity, recommendation, prediction, or favorable risk/reward profile.

Alerts simply notify users that a selected condition has occurred.
