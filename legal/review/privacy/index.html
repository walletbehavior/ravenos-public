01

Scope and controller details

This candidate applies to RavenOS websites, applications, accounts, public profiles, intelligence, trading interfaces, wallet features, Copy, referrals, and related support. It does not govern independent blockchains, wallets, venues, protocols, routers, bridges, authentication providers, or other third parties.

The approved policy must identify the RavenOS legal entity responsible for the relevant processing, its address, privacy contact, and any representative or data-protection officer required by applicable law. Those details remain unresolved in this draft.

02

Information processed

Account and authentication

RavenOS receives a verified email address, managed-provider subject identifier, authentication method, opaque internal user ID, user-selected username, account state, and authentication timestamps. RavenOS uses the username—not first or family name from the provider—as the default public identity. Authentication providers process credentials under their own policies; RavenOS does not receive a Google password.

Security and device records

RavenOS processes opaque session and CSRF identifiers, one-time sign-in state, coarse device labels, session creation, activity, expiry and revocation times, bounded security events, and rate-limit data. The current rate limiter transforms a coarse network prefix with a keyed one-way function rather than storing a full address in its customer tables. Edge and infrastructure providers may process network and request metadata independently.

Account preferences and product records

RavenOS may store usernames, privacy controls, public-profile settings, follows and positive recognition, saved markets, watch items, alerts, selected chart settings, Pro entitlement evidence, referral attribution, legal-document acceptances, optional wallet associations, and requested support activity.

Trading and intelligence

For supported features, RavenOS may process canonical asset and instrument identifiers, chain and venue, public wallet addresses, balances, quotes, previews, policy decisions, transaction or order references, execution receipts, reconciliation state, portfolio snapshots, Copy settings and signals, paper or shadow outcomes, and provider/evidence timestamps. Secret keys, seed phrases, and raw wallet recovery material must not be stored in RavenOS product databases.

Billing

If Raven Pro checkout is activated, the payment processor—not RavenOS application code—should collect payment-card details. RavenOS may receive customer, subscription, status, invoice, refund, chargeback, tax, and product identifiers needed for billing and entitlements. Checkout is not active in the audited build.

03

Wallet and public-blockchain data

Blockchains are public ledgers. RavenOS analyzes public addresses, transactions, logs, token balances, pools, positions, and related market evidence whether or not the address belongs to a Raven user. Public-chain analysis does not by itself identify a natural person.

If you connect an external wallet, RavenOS processes its public address, chain, connection and signing state, and the transaction data needed for the action you request. If you voluntarily verify wallet control, RavenOS stores the address, challenge and verification result. Public association with a username remains off unless you explicitly enable it.

For optional Privy embedded wallets, RavenOS stores the Raven-to-Privy association, Privy wallet identifier, ecosystem, and public address. Privy processes authentication, wallet creation, signing, and any export or recovery flow under its own terms. The current RavenOS spike does not store private keys and has not yet verified production export, recovery, withdrawal, or delegated signing end-to-end.

04

Purposes of processing

RavenOS uses information to create and protect accounts; provide requested pages, analytics, wallet and trading functions; build evidence packets and receipts; enforce policies and limits; prevent duplicate or unauthorized actions; reconcile venue state; provide support; maintain security and reliability; measure provider and product health; administer subscriptions or referrals when active; and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Public-chain observations may be normalized once and shared across product consumers so RavenOS does not duplicate expensive provider requests. User-specific settings, balances, allocations, Copy activity, and execution history must remain tenant-scoped.

The audited build contains no third-party behavioral-advertising tracker. Product instrumentation is designed for service health, security, and bounded usage metrics. If advertising, cross-site tracking, or a new analytics provider is introduced, the policy and consent mechanism must be reassessed before activation.

05

What becomes public

An account and username are private by default. A public Raven profile is separately opt-in. Depending on the settings you enable, a profile may show username, membership date, percentage performance, indexed equity curves, risk metrics, markets or chains used, strategy summaries, follows, public watchlists, verified-wallet status, or Copy availability.

RavenOS does not publish your email, legal name, billing identity, geographic information, account dollar balance, private strategy settings, follower capital, Copy allocation, or wallet addresses by default. Turning on a public setting does not make unrelated fields public.

Public content and blockchain data can be copied, indexed, or retained by others. Removing an association from RavenOS cannot erase the underlying chain record or copies held outside RavenOS.

06

Service providers and disclosures

The current architecture uses or stages providers in these categories: managed authentication (WorkOS and selected identity providers), edge hosting and database services (Cloudflare), embedded-wallet infrastructure (Privy), market and blockchain data (including RPC, index, chart, and discovery providers), execution routing (including Jupiter and 0x), venues (including Hyperliquid), and future payment processing (Stripe scaffold).

RavenOS sends each provider the data reasonably needed for the requested function, such as an authenticated subject, public wallet address, chain, token, route request, or transaction status. RavenOS may also disclose information to investigate abuse, protect users and systems, respond to lawful process, enforce agreements, complete a business transaction subject to appropriate safeguards, or with your direction.

The approved policy must include a verified processor inventory, international-transfer mechanisms where applicable, and any legally required “sale,” “sharing,” targeted-advertising, or sensitive-data disclosures. This draft does not assume those legal classifications.

07

Cookies and local storage

RavenOS uses strictly functional secure cookies for one-time authentication state, the server session, and CSRF protection. These are necessary for account security and are not advertising cookies.

The browser may store functional preferences such as selected market context, Terminal ticket settings, chart display choices, and a Discover first-visit marker. External wallet or embedded-wallet SDKs may use their own browser storage to maintain a connection or provider session. RavenOS should not store private keys, session bearer tokens, email addresses, or raw signing material in local storage.

A separate cookie-consent banner is not proposed for the audited functional-only implementation. Counsel must reassess this if non-essential analytics, advertising, or cross-site technologies are added.

08

Retention and deletion

RavenOS retains information for the time needed to provide the feature, protect the service, satisfy accounting and legal obligations, resolve disputes, and preserve append-only audit integrity. Session state expires or is revoked; short-lived quotes and auth state use bounded expiries; saved items remain until removed; public-chain and market evidence may be retained as shared historical evidence.

Legal acceptances, execution receipts, security events, billing evidence, and audit records may need longer retention than ordinary preferences. Deleting an account may remove or de-identify active profile and preference data while preserving records that RavenOS is legally required or reasonably needs to retain. Backups age out under separate schedules.

The audited product does not yet implement self-service account deletion or a complete record-by-record retention schedule. Both are pre-launch legal and engineering items. Until then, requests use the support channel displayed inside RavenOS.

09

Your controls and privacy rights

You can sign out, revoke sessions, choose a username, remove saved markets and alerts, disconnect supported external wallets, keep Community private, and choose granular public-profile settings. Supported embedded-wallet withdrawal, export, and recovery controls must remain available even if you decline updated trading terms, subject to authentication, security, and provider availability.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or receive a copy of certain personal information, and to appeal a denied request. Exceptions may apply to public-chain data, security records, legal obligations, and records RavenOS cannot technically control. The approved policy must identify the request and appeal channels, verification process, response time, and region-specific rights.

10

Security boundaries

RavenOS uses managed authentication, exact callback and origin rules, one-time state, PKCE, host-only secure cookies, opaque sessions, rotation, revocation, CSRF controls, bounded inputs, tenant checks, server-side secrets, idempotency, and append-only records where appropriate. No online system or wallet can guarantee absolute security.

Never give RavenOS or another person your seed phrase or private key. Review the chain, wallet, token, destination, approval, amount, fees, and transaction before signing. Contact support promptly if you suspect account misuse, but do not include secrets in a support message.

11

International processing, children, and restrictions

Providers may process information in the United States and other countries. Privacy protections can differ. The approved policy must identify the contracting entity’s locations and any transfer mechanisms required for the initial launch geographies.

RavenOS is not intended for children. The proposed minimum age is 18. The audited account flow does not yet enforce age or obtain parental consent; broad account and trading availability must wait for an approved eligibility control.

RavenOS may restrict service based on law, sanctions, product availability, provider terms, or unresolved risk. Geographic and sanctions controls remain a separate compliance workstream and must not be inferred from this policy.

12

Policy updates and contact

RavenOS versions and content-hashes legal documents. Material updates may require new acknowledgement before affected new activity. An update to trading or Copy terms does not by itself prevent account access or supported wallet withdrawal, export, or recovery.

The approved policy must state its effective date and the RavenOS legal entity’s privacy and support contacts. Until then, use the support channel displayed in the application. Do not send passwords, seed phrases, private keys, recovery material, or full authorization tokens.

Trial and rewards records

Trial eligibility uses server-side keyed digests of verified authentication identities and email addresses, prior trial and paid history, and existing security state. Wallet addresses are not the sole eligibility signal. Raven does not introduce device fingerprinting for trials. Reward accounting records link an account to execution fee evidence, wallet-destination verification, claim settlements, and subscription-credit records. Operational events measure trial and rewards use under the existing privacy policy; they are not a new third-party advertising feed. Financial and anti-abuse retention periods require approval in the final retention schedule.
